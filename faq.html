How long does shipping take?

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Express delivery is available in major cities.

What is your return policy?

Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery in their original condition.

We currently ship within the country only. International shipping is coming soon.

How do I track my order?

You'll receive a tracking link by email as soon as your order leaves our warehouse.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes, within one hour of placing it. Contact support with your order number.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Major credit and debit cards, plus popular digital wallets at checkout.
